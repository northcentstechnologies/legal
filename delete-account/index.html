Delete your NorthCents account

Last updated: May 1, 2026 · Operated by Jayden Player as NorthCents, Calgary, Alberta, Canada.

We make it easy to delete your NorthCents account and associated personal data at any time. You do not need to keep the app installed to submit a deletion request.

Important — your transactions stay on your device. NorthCents keeps all of your financial transactions, receipts, budgets, and categories on your phone (or tablet). Account deletion removes the server-side information listed below. To also wipe the on-device data, uninstall the app or use the in-app Reset all data option under Settings → Privacy Center before uninstalling.

Option 1 — Delete in the app (fastest, under 30 seconds)

  1. Open the NorthCents app.
  2. Go to the Account tab (bottom right).
  3. Tap Privacy Center → Delete my account.
  4. Confirm your decision. Your account is scheduled for deletion immediately and all server-side data is purged within 30 days.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email

If you no longer have access to the app, email us and we will process your deletion request within 30 days as required by PIPEDA, Alberta PIPA, GDPR, and CCPA.

Email deletion request Privacy Policy

Include the following in your email

  • The email address you used to sign up for NorthCents.
  • The platform you used the app on (iOS or Android).
  • Approximate date you signed up (if you remember).

We may ask you to verify your identity by replying from the same email address on file. This prevents someone else from deleting your account without your permission.

What will be deleted

Data Deleted? Timeline
Account record (email, name, phone, address, password hash) Yes Within 30 days
Subscription record and RevenueCat customer ID Yes Within 30 days
Referral rewards history tied to your account Yes Within 30 days
Access logs and security events tied to your account Yes Within 30 days
Financial transactions, receipts, budgets, categories Never stored on our servers On-device — remove by uninstalling or using Reset all data
Anonymous crash reports (opt-in only) Already anonymized — not linked to your account N/A

What we are legally required to keep

We retain a small amount of data after deletion for tax, fraud prevention, and legal-compliance purposes, as required by Canadian law:

  • Subscription payment records (invoice amount, date, anonymized customer ID) for 7 years — mandatory under the Canada Revenue Agency record-keeping rules.
  • Abuse and fraud logs if your account was associated with a terms-of-service violation.

These records contain no financial-transaction data from the app itself and cannot be used to reconstruct your personal spending.
