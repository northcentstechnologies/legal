1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy describes how the NorthCents mobile application collects, uses, and protects information about its users.

NorthCents is currently operated as a sole proprietorship by Jayden Player, located at 1047 Mahogany Boulevard SE, Calgary, Alberta T3M 3X5, Canada. The operator, referred to throughout this document as "we," "us," or "NorthCents," is the data controller for any personal information processed through the NorthCents application and related services.

For any privacy-related question, request, or complaint, please contact us at northcentstechnologies@gmail.com. We aim to respond to all privacy requests within 30 days. Once NorthCents Technologies, Inc. is incorporated, this policy will be amended to reflect the corporate entity; we will announce any such change through the app and by email to registered users.

2. Summary for people who do not want to read the full document

NorthCents is built to minimize the amount of personal data we hold about you. Financial information — including every transaction amount, category, receipt photo, note, and budget — is stored only on your device and never transmitted to our servers.

We collect and store on our servers only what is strictly necessary to operate an account: your email address, the legal name you provide, your telephone number, your postal address, a short subscription status record, and server-side logs related to authentication and webhook events. When you subscribe through Google Play or the Apple App Store, the store, along with RevenueCat (our subscription-management processor), receives the information that it needs to process the subscription. We never receive your payment card details, bank credentials, or the detailed contents of your purchases.

We do not sell personal information. We do not share your data with advertisers. We do not build behavioural profiles of you. You can export your data or delete your account at any time from within the app.

3. Information we collect and why

3.1 Information you provide directly

When you create an account in NorthCents, we ask you to sign in through Manus OAuth using Email, Google, or Apple Sign-In. Regardless of the sign-in method, we receive and store your verified email address. After authentication, we ask you to complete a short profile with your full name, telephone number, and mailing address. These four data points are collectively referred to in this policy as "account information."

We collect account information so that we can identify you across devices, restore your subscription entitlement after reinstallation, send important service notices (for example, a warning that your subscription renewal has failed), and comply with consumer-protection and anti-fraud obligations applicable to paid digital subscriptions. Collection of name, telephone, and address is also a common requirement from payment processors for chargeback defence and for tax-documentation purposes.

3.2 Information generated on your device and stored locally

When you use NorthCents, the app creates and maintains a local database on your phone that contains all of your financial records. This includes transaction amounts, dates, categories, notes, receipts photographed or imported into the app, budget limits, savings goals, recurring-payment schedules, and any bank-account information you import through Plaid. Biometric authentication keys, encryption keys for your on-device backup, and your preference for crash-report opt-in are also stored locally in the operating system's secure keystore.

None of this financial or behavioural data leaves your device unless you explicitly trigger a feature that requires it to, such as requesting a data export, creating an encrypted backup that you choose to share, or granting the operating system permission to let a third-party app read a shared file.

3.3 Subscription status

When you purchase a subscription through the Google Play Store or Apple App Store, the store issues a receipt to the app. A small record of this receipt is synchronised to our server so we can confirm your subscription entitlement when you sign in on a new device. This record contains the product identifier (for example, "premium_yearly"), the subscription start and end dates, the store of origin, and an anonymous identifier generated by RevenueCat. It does not contain your payment card number, your name as it appears on the card, your billing address as it appears on the card, or any information about the individual purchases you make inside the app.

3.4 Technical information collected automatically

When the app communicates with our server — for sign-in, profile updates, subscription syncing, data export, or account deletion — our server automatically records an access log entry consisting of the request timestamp, the requested endpoint, the HTTP response code, and an abbreviated version of the IP address from which the request originated. We keep these logs for 30 days for abuse-prevention, rate-limiting, and security-incident investigation; after 30 days, they are automatically deleted. We do not use these logs for advertising, marketing, profiling, or analytics purposes.

3.5 Diagnostic and crash information (optional, opt-in)

NorthCents includes an integration with Sentry, a third-party crash-reporting service. This integration is disabled by default. If you explicitly opt in from the Privacy Center, Sentry will receive automatically-scrubbed crash traces and non-fatal error events from the app. The payload contains the stack trace, the app version, the operating-system version, the device model, and a randomly-generated install identifier. It does not contain your transactions, receipts, categories, or account information. You can revoke this consent at any time from the same screen, after which no further data is transmitted.

4. How we use your information

We use the information described above for a small, closed set of purposes.

Account information is used to authenticate you, personalise the greeting in the app, restore your subscription entitlement across devices, issue tax-related communications where legally required, and send transactional messages about subscription status. We do not use your email address for marketing unless you separately opt in, and we do not currently operate any marketing list.

Subscription status information is used to unlock or lock premium features within the app and to reconcile with the Google Play and Apple App Store transaction records for refund handling and fraud detection.

Technical logs are used exclusively for security, abuse prevention, and debugging our own server software. Crash reports, if you opt in, are used exclusively to identify and fix defects in the app.

We do not use your personal information for automated decision-making or profiling that would produce legal or similarly significant effects on you.

5. Legal bases for processing (for users in the European Economic Area and United Kingdom)

Where the General Data Protection Regulation applies to you, we rely on the following legal bases. The processing of account information is necessary for the performance of the service contract between you and us. The processing of subscription status is also contractual and, in addition, necessary for compliance with our legal obligations regarding consumer-protection and tax reporting. The processing of security and technical logs is based on our legitimate interest in operating a secure service, balanced against your privacy interests, which we consider to be adequately preserved by retention limits and the abbreviation of IP addresses. The processing of optional crash-report data is based on your freely-given, specific, informed, and revocable consent.

6. Your rights

We recognise the rights granted to you by applicable law, including the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA), Alberta's Personal Information Protection Act, the General Data Protection Regulation (for users in the EEA/UK), and the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act.

You have the right to access the personal information we hold about you, to correct inaccuracies in that information, to request its deletion, to receive a copy of it in a portable machine-readable format, to restrict or object to certain processing activities, to withdraw consent where consent is the legal basis, and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

In the app, the Account screen exposes two buttons that satisfy most of these rights directly and immediately:

  • Export My Data produces a JSON archive containing your account information and your on-server subscription record. Because your transaction data is stored only on your device, the exporter also reads your local database and appends a CSV representation of every transaction, budget, goal, and receipt metadata entry to the archive. No human is involved in the export; the archive is generated on demand and delivered through the operating system share sheet.
  • Delete My Account irreversibly deletes your account on our server, together with your account information, subscription record, and server-side logs tied to your user identifier. After a 30-day grace period intended to allow you to reverse an accidental deletion, we permanently erase backups. Upon deletion you are also offered the option to wipe the local database on the current device. Subscription status on the Google Play Store or Apple App Store itself must be cancelled through the respective store; we cannot cancel store-side subscriptions on your behalf.

If you prefer to exercise these rights by email, contact northcentstechnologies@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days. We may ask you to confirm your identity before processing a request.

6.1 California-specific disclosures

Residents of California have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act. Specifically, you have the right to know the categories of personal information collected, the purposes for which it is used, and the categories of recipients with whom it is shared; the right to request deletion of personal information; and the right to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising as those terms are defined under California law. The "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" requirement therefore does not apply to NorthCents; we have chosen not to engage in such activities at all.

6.2 Alberta and Canada-specific disclosures

Residents of Alberta and elsewhere in Canada have the right to access their personal information and to request correction under PIPEDA and Alberta's Personal Information Protection Act. The in-app export and deletion controls described above, together with the privacy contact email, fully support these rights. If you are unsatisfied with our response to a privacy request, you may contact the Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner of Alberta or the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

7. Who we share information with

We share information only with the limited number of third parties required to provide the service, and only to the extent necessary. We do not sell, rent, or otherwise disclose personal information for any purpose other than what is described below.

Google Play Store and Apple App Store receive the information that they need to process your subscription purchase. The exact data they receive is governed by their own privacy policies (Google Play and Apple). Neither store receives the contents of your transactions, receipts, notes, or budgets.

RevenueCat, Inc. is our subscription-management processor. RevenueCat receives a pseudonymous application user identifier (a UUID generated by NorthCents), the product identifier you purchased, and the receipt issued by the store. RevenueCat uses this information to validate receipts against the store servers, detect fraud, and notify our server when your subscription status changes. RevenueCat's processing is governed by its Privacy Policy. RevenueCat is headquartered in the United States.

Manus Technology Inc. provides the OAuth authentication service that signs you into NorthCents. When you sign in, Manus receives your email address and returns a session token to the app. Manus's processing is governed by its privacy policy.

Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.) receives crash-report data only if you have explicitly opted in through the Privacy Center. Sentry's processing is governed by its Privacy Policy. Sentry is headquartered in the United States.

Plaid Inc. — if you choose to use the optional bank-import feature, Plaid receives the credentials that you enter for your bank and returns transaction data to the app. Transaction data returned from Plaid is stored only on your device; it is never transmitted to our server. Plaid's processing is governed by its Privacy Policy. Plaid is headquartered in the United States and also operates in Canada.

Law enforcement or regulators may receive information in response to a valid legal process. We will challenge requests that are overbroad or that do not comply with applicable law, and we will notify affected users unless legally prohibited from doing so.

Except as described above, we do not share personal information with any third party.

8. International data transfers

NorthCents is operated from Canada, and our servers are located in data centres operated by our infrastructure provider inside the United States and Canada. RevenueCat, Sentry, Plaid, and Manus also host infrastructure primarily in the United States. When personal information is transferred from Canada or from the European Economic Area to the United States, we rely on contractual safeguards equivalent to the Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission. Personal information leaving Alberta is subject to foreign jurisdictions' laws; by using NorthCents you acknowledge this transfer.

9. Retention

We retain account information for as long as your account is active. After you delete your account, we retain a copy for 30 days to allow you to reverse an accidental deletion, after which the record is permanently erased from our databases and from our backup media at the next scheduled purge. Security and technical logs are retained for 30 days and then automatically deleted. Crash reports, where opted in, are retained by Sentry for 90 days in accordance with its default policy. Subscription records are retained for the longer of the duration required by tax law in the seller's jurisdiction, which is typically six years in Canada, or the duration required by the Google Play and Apple App Store developer agreements.

On-device data is retained by your device only and is under your sole control. Uninstalling NorthCents or clearing the app's storage permanently deletes all on-device data.

10. Security

We apply industry-standard safeguards to protect personal information. All communication between the app and our servers occurs over TLS 1.2 or higher. Passwords are never transmitted or stored in plaintext; authentication uses OAuth tokens issued by Manus. Subscription secrets transmitted between RevenueCat and our server are verified using a HMAC-SHA256 signature. Biometric-protected data on your device is sealed by your operating system's secure keystore and is not accessible to us. On-device backups created through the Advanced Settings screen are encrypted with a key derived from a password that you choose and that we do not know.

No security regime is perfect, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. If we become aware of a breach affecting your personal information, we will notify you and the appropriate authorities without undue delay, in accordance with applicable law.

11. Children

NorthCents is a personal-finance application intended for adult users. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of thirteen, and the app is not directed at such children. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at northcentstechnologies@gmail.com and we will promptly delete the information.
